# BRICKGRADE × GOATVERSE — “THE CARDS DRAFT US”

Original 60-second animated launch commercial for DJ Speedy, Waka Flocka
Flame, BrickGrade Exchange, and GOAT FORCE.

## The hook

People think they play the cards. In the GOATVERSE, the cards are alive—and
they draft Speedy and Waka. A freshly sealed BrickGrade card opens portals to
original creature champions, pirate crews, hoop legends, and multiverse
strategists. Each world tries to recruit the two GOAT commanders for a
reality-bending championship. The final play is not an attack: it is proof.
The winning card returns to the BrickGrade calibration vault, where evidence,
QC, and owner approval lock the result.

Official Pokémon, One Piece, NBA, or other licensed characters, team marks,
uniforms, logos, music, and card art are not included in the original master.
The worlds below are original and commercially usable once final art, voices,
music, likeness releases, and legal clearance are approved. Licensed crossover
assets can be substituted later under a partnership agreement.

## Visual system

- Real grading lane: neutral macro light, true color, no beauty retouching,
  timestamped evidence overlays, restrained cyan interface.
- Synthetic reveal lane: violet/gold energy, impossible camera moves, volumetric
  arenas, animated foil response, and a permanent “GOATVERSE SYNTHETIC” slate.
- Transition language: card borders become portals; centering guides become
  stadium lines; foil patterns become oceans, skies, and crowd energy.
- Speedy/Waka likeness: stylized original digital doubles only after written
  likeness and voice approval. Never train or publish a double from unapproved
  source media.

## Production routing

1. Capture the real card front, back, edges, foil response, and grade evidence.
2. Lock the immutable evidence folder before any generative processing.
3. Build the card digital twin in Blender/Unreal with a physically based foil
   material and calibrated texture maps.
4. Generate original creature, pirate, and Hoopverse motion passes through the
   approved local model rack or an explicitly connected provider.
5. Assemble characters, cameras, lighting, and card interaction in Unreal;
   use Twinmotion only where it improves environment look development.
6. Export EXR/PNG sequences, camera data, mattes, and guide audio.
7. Finish in DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, or After Effects; keep a clean
   synthetic-content disclosure in every publish master.
8. Rehearse in OBS/private RTMP. Speedy or Waka gives the final broadcast and
   publish approval.

## Trust rule

Generated or retouched frames are entertainment assets only. They never enter
the condition score, evidence passport, authenticity decision, comparable-sale
record, or QC result. The commercial shows this separation on screen: proof
first, imagination second.
